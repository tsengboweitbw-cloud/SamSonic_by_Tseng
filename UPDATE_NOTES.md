# Update notes

## 2.2.3

- When editing a playlist, a song can be held anywhere on its row to drag it to another place, not only by the handle on the right, so the last song can still be moved when a bar covers its handle (`EditSongRow`). Dragging near the top or bottom now scrolls the list faster and from further in (120 dp, up to 30 dp a frame, speeding up towards the edge), so a held song can be taken through a long playlist quickly (`PlaylistDragState`). A song can also be held with one hand while the other hand scrolls the list: a second finger put down anywhere scrolls and flings it, and the held song stays under the first finger, swapping places as rows pass (`scrollWithSecondFinger`). Other songs ignore touches while one is held. The auto-scroll tops out at 30 dp a frame, and a swap at the top of the list no longer makes the whole list jump a row (it held its place by the key of the first visible row, which moves with the dragged song). The song under your finger is now placed from where the finger is each frame, so it cannot slip away from it. Leaving editing after saving keeps the list on the song it was on, even when the saved playlist arrives a moment later, instead of jumping to the top. After a new order is saved, the saved rows now swap in for the edit rows in one frame, on the same song, with no fade or flash while it syncs with the server. The other hand's scroll and fling while dragging are now applied right after each swap, so a fling no longer stutters when the held song is near the top. Rows now slide into place faster during a drag, and a swap can no longer leave the held song stuck away from the finger. Flinging up with a song held no longer makes it flicker back and forth with the row above or freeze the list.

- Home can be set up the way you like it: Settings has a new Home group with a Home sections menu, where each section (Picked for you, Recently added, Recently played and Most played, albums and songs) can be shown or hidden with its switch, set to show from 1 up to what it fetched (60 albums, 99 songs) with a slider, and moved up or down with its arrows; Reset to default undoes it all. Home follows at once, and says so when every section is hidden. Hidden sections are still fetched with the rest (`HomeLayoutManager`, `HomeSettings`, `HomeScreen`).

- Auto DJ can now leave things out as well as keep to them: tap a genre, decade or artist once to pick only those, twice to never pick them.
- Auto DJ's settings are tidier, with every option kept. The Genres, Decades and Artists filters are rows that open on a tap and show what is chosen (the first, then "+2") while closed, with Clear once open; before, all their chips were always showing. "Follow the song playing" is one row of three chips (Same artist, Same genre, Same era) rather than three switch rows, and the two hints that went with them are folded into the note beneath (`FilterTitle` in `AutoDjFilterRows`, `autoDjSettingsItems`).

- The USB DAC driver and Native DSD are now on by default. A DAC that can't take a format falls back step by step (native DSD, then DoP, then PCM at a rate it supports), and one the driver can't read is left to Android, so nothing needs switching on first. A choice already made in Settings is kept (`UsbDacManager`).

- Long-pressing a playlist in Library now opens a menu with Save for offline (or Remove from offline once all its songs are kept) as well as Delete, instead of going straight to the delete question. Delete still asks first, as a second step in the same card, and Cancel returns to the menu. Favourites can be saved for offline but not deleted; without a server in use the menu shows only Delete (`DeletePlaylistMenu`, `deleteOnLongPress` in `LibraryScreen`).

- Long-pressing a playlist in Library now opens its menu like the other long-press menus: the nav bar and mini player dim with the page, and tapping either closes the menu instead of expanding Now Playing behind it. The menu had not said it was over the floating chrome (`LocalMenuBottomInset` in `LibraryScreen`).

- The long-press menus offer Play next as well as Add to queue: on a song or album (`AddToPlaylistMenu`, not shown for the song already playing) and on a playlist in Library, which now offers both ahead of Save for offline and Delete (`DeletePlaylistMenu`).

- A playlist's cover now syncs both ways with Navidrome. Saving a cover while editing uploads it to the server (Navidrome's own API: `/auth/login`, then `/api/playlist/{id}/image`), so other devices and clients show it too, and removing it removes it there. If the server won't take the upload, the cover is kept on this phone as before (`setPlaylistCover` / `removePlaylistCover` in `MusicLibrary`, `SubsonicRepository`).

## 2.2.2

- Long-pressing a song or album opens a menu of what you can do with it, instead of going straight to the playlists: Add to queue, Add to playlist, Go to album, Go to album artist, Go to artist, and Save for offline (with Remove from this playlist first in a playlist, and Try saving again where a song was refused). Where there is a choice to make, it is a second step in the same glass card, and Back returns to the actions: Add to playlist shows the playlists and New playlist, and Go to artist lists the artists when a song has several (with one it goes straight there). An album offers only Go to artist. Go to album artist reads it from the song, else from its album when tapped. The pages open over the tab that is showing, or after Now Playing folds away when the menu was opened from there (`AddToPlaylistMenu`, `Step.Playlists` and `Step.Artists`). Only the album's one artist is known to the app, so the album artist step has no list.

- The mini player's glass no longer sometimes blurs the wrong part of the page (the patch behind where the pill had been, rather than right below it). Haze reads where the glass sits only when it is laid out, and the sheet's own layer moves the pill (coming in, swiped away, lifted in the card pile) without a layout pass; the glass now reads those in its placement (`SheetSurface` in `PlayerSheet.kt`), so each frame of a move places it again.

## 2.2.1

- The USB DAC driver now takes over a queue that is already loaded: switching it on in Settings, plugging a DAC in or allowing its permission while songs are queued sets the current song up again on the DAC (and switching it off or unplugging does the same for Android's output), where before the queue kept playing the old way until it was replaced. The song and position are kept (`UsbDacManager.ready`, watched in `PlaybackService`).

It shows on Auto DJ's page in Settings and in its panel in Now Playing.

The photo then opens in a full-screen cropper (`CoverCropDialog`): drag to move it, pinch to zoom up to 8 times, the photo always covering the square; Done shows the crop at once, kept on Save (Cancel, here or in the page, drops it). A cross at its corner lets a picked or saved cover go, back to the server's own. The picture is cropped to a square and kept on the phone (`PlaylistCovers`, `files/playlist_covers`), because a Subsonic server has no way to set one, so it shows only on this phone and per server; it replaces the cover everywhere the playlist appears (Library, Home, search, the playlist page). ## 2.1.0

- Bit-perfect USB output is gone, and with it the Exclusive USB output and DSD output settings: Android 14's bit-perfect mixer attributes (`AudioMixerAttributes`) never worked reliably, so the app no longer asks Android for them. The integer and native-DSD AudioTracks, the in-app resampler to the DAC's rate, DoP and native DSD packing, and the Exclusive line in Song info and Now Playing went with them, and the player is the stock one on every Android version again. DSD files (DSF, DSDIFF) still play, always filtered to PCM in the app. The `MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS` permission is dropped. A bit-perfect driver of our own may come later.
- USB DAC driver (experimental, off by default; Settings > Playback > USB DAC driver): the app now drives a plugged-in USB DAC itself, in native code (libusb over usbfs, USB audio class 1 and 2), instead of asking Android to. Songs go out bit for bit, in the DAC's own sample size and at the song's own rate where the DAC takes it (resampled to the nearest rate it does take otherwise), with gapless trimming, seeking and pause working as before, and the DAC's own volume. DSD files go to the DAC as DoP, or with the new Native DSD switch, in the DAC's raw-data format when it has one, and the DAC is sent DSD silence in between so it doesn't drop out of DSD. Without a DAC, or with the switch off, everything plays as before. A DAC unplugged mid-song pauses it. While the driver plays, Now Playing's info capsule and Song info show what it sends: the rate and sample size of PCM (or DSD64, DSD128... with DoP or Native), the USB DAC's name, and the rate its clock runs at. New native code: `app/src/main/cpp` (with libusb 1.0.29 vendored).
- A USB DAC the driver can't read, such as Apple's USB-C headphone adapter (it presents itself as USB audio class 3), is left to Android: the driver checks a DAC's descriptors before taking it over, so its songs play as usual instead of falling silent.
- New defaults, for a fresh install or a setting never changed: the Like button is off, the Audio format shows by the duration, the Play buttons pin at the top, album art is rounded 3dp (was 2dp), and the player background, Now Playing controls and menu blurs are 16dp like the navigation bar's (were 40dp, 48dp and 28dp). A setting you've already changed keeps its value.
- A value too long for Now Playing's info window, such as a USB DAC's name, now scrolls as a marquee in its row instead of running off the card.
- The back button's glass now matches the nav bar and mini player: it takes the same accent sheen and the same chrome blur as the nav bar, where before it had only the blur and tint.
- The Play, Shuffle and Queue buttons above a list take the navigation bar's opacity setting like the other capsules, rather than the denser card opacity.
- Now Playing's pop-up panels (Lyrics, Up next, Song info, Auto DJ) take the Now Playing controls' blur and opacity settings, like the controls, rather than the menus' settings, and lose the accent sheen the menus have.
- The Now Playing controls' opacity slider now runs from 50% to 130% like the other opacity settings, as a multiplier over the old 70% look (100% is what 70% was). Anyone who had changed it starts again at 100%.
- Now Playing's pop-up panels (Up next and the rest) no longer stretch their glass on the bounce at the end of opening: the glass is laid out with room for the overshoot, so the slightly larger bounds show the real blur of what's behind them at every moment.
