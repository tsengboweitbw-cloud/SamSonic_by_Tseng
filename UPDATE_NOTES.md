# Update notes

## 2.1.0 (in progress)

- Bit-perfect USB output is gone, and with it the Exclusive USB output and DSD output settings: Android 14's bit-perfect mixer attributes (`AudioMixerAttributes`) never worked reliably, so the app no longer asks Android for them. The integer and native-DSD AudioTracks, the in-app resampler to the DAC's rate, DoP and native DSD packing, and the Exclusive line in Song info and Now Playing went with them, and the player is the stock one on every Android version again. DSD files (DSF, DSDIFF) still play, always filtered to PCM in the app. The `MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS` permission is dropped. A bit-perfect driver of our own may come later.
- The back button's glass now matches the nav bar and mini player: it takes the same accent sheen and the same chrome blur as the nav bar, where before it had only the blur and tint.
- The Play, Shuffle and Queue buttons above a list take the navigation bar's opacity setting like the other capsules, rather than the denser card opacity.
- Now Playing's pop-up panels (Lyrics, Up next, Song info, Auto DJ) take the Now Playing controls' blur and opacity settings, like the controls, rather than the menus' settings, and lose the accent sheen the menus have.
- The Now Playing controls' opacity slider now runs from 50% to 130% like the other opacity settings, as a multiplier over the old 70% look (100% is what 70% was). Anyone who had changed it starts again at 100%.
- Now Playing's pop-up panels (Up next and the rest) no longer stretch their glass on the bounce at the end of opening: the glass is laid out with room for the overshoot, so the slightly larger bounds show the real blur of what's behind them at every moment.
- On a phone on its side, the navigation rail is narrower (68dp, not 88dp, widening only if a label needs it) and sits 8dp from the edge, not 16dp, so the pages beside it have more room on the left. It is shorter too, with smaller gaps around it, so it fits a 360dp-tall screen without crowding.
- On a phone on its side, the navigation rail now uses the height the circular mini player frees: its tabs grow to fill the room between the status bar and the mini player (at most the normal tab height) instead of a fixed 32dp.
- Now Playing's four audio info lines (format, output, device, device rate) are now one small capsule, the file's format (about 20dp tall, with a chevron). Tapping it opens a small window like the lyrics one: it grows out of the capsule, sits at the capsule's place over a dimmed Now Playing (the layout does not change), and lists all four lines (format, output, device, device rate) as four rows, each with its icon, name and value; tapping outside or back folds it into the capsule. The window's text is short: units without spaces (96kHz, 24bit), stereo left unsaid, and the device rate as just the rate. Song info keeps the full wording.
- On a phone on its side, the mini player under the rail is now just the cover in a circle, with the progress running round it; a tap opens Now Playing and a long press plays or pauses. There is no play/pause button in this layout.
- The Up next card has a Clear queue button above the Auto DJ strip. While music plays, it removes every song but the current one; paused, it removes them all, stops playback and closes Now Playing and the mini player, like swiping the mini player away.
- The Library tab bar is no wider than 448dp (a side pane at its widest), kept to the left, so on a tablet it no longer stretches across the screen when nothing is open beside it.
- Beside the navigation rail, sliding a finger along it now fades the page with it: the page fades out as the indicator leaves a tab and the next fades in as it arrives, rather than nothing moving until you let go.
- Two new Library settings, Album names and Album artist names, hide the name or the artist under each album's cover in the Albums tab and the album pages opened from it (grid and list). Both are on by default.
- Sliding a finger along the nav bar (and the rail) now holds each tab's label fully open and opaque for a stretch as the indicator passes (from 60% of the way in to the centre), with a shorter edge fade, so a swipe that doesn't stop on a tab no longer shows its label always fading in or out.

Changes since 5dfb5ba.


- README: the version badge read v2.0.0 and now reads v2.0.5, the latest release. The Releases, badge and clone links pointed at the old `TsengBoWei/SamSonic` repository (404); they now point at `tsengboweitbw-cloud/SamSonic_by_Tseng`, and the APK name matches the published `Samsonic-by-Tseng-V<version>.apk`.
- Tablet mode, phase 0 (branch `tablet-mode`; no visible change yet): the app now works out a layout class from its window's size (`ui/common/LayoutClass.kt`: phone under 600dp wide or 480dp tall, one pane from 600dp, two panes from 700dp wide and 600dp tall, never from portrait or landscape), provided to every screen as `LocalWindowLayout`, with the fold posture for later. Adds `material3-adaptive` 1.3.0.
- Turning the phone, split screen and folding no longer restart the screens (the manifest's `configChanges`), so what's open stays open. Before Android 13 the activity restarted on any such change it was told of; it now restarts only when the phone's language changes.
- When the activity does start over (the system closing it in the background, the phone switching dark mode), Now Playing reopens if it was open, Search keeps its query, and a source switch afterwards still stays in Settings.
- Tablet mode, phase 1: on a screen wider than a phone (600dp and up), the nav bar becomes a rail at the side (`GlassNavRail`): the same glass and gliding indicator standing on end, the selected tab growing taller with its label sliding out below its icon, and a finger sliding along it carrying the indicator. The tabs' pages sit beside it, and a switch fades from one tab to the next rather than sliding. It slides out of the way as Now Playing opens, as the nav bar sinks. Folding a foldable shut brings the nav bar back on the cover screen.
- Beside the rail, the mini player sits alone at the foot of the screen, centred in the room past the rail and no wider than 600dp. A menu open over a page dims the rail too, and a tap on it closes the menu.
- Stack nav bar and mini player is dimmed on wider screens (there's no nav bar at the foot to pile onto); a tap says it's only on phones. The setting is kept for a phone or a folded foldable.
- Settings in two columns on a tablet or an open foldable (700dp and wider): the groups (Music source, Playback, Library, Appearance, Storage, About) listed in a card at the side, and the one picked shown beside it; the pick is kept as the screen turns. Narrower, Settings stays one page of every group in turn.
- The nav rail is longer: each tab 68dp tall rather than 56dp, so it stands taller at the side of a big screen.
- Picking another group in two-column Settings now moves: the highlight glides to it down the list (with the rail's soft spring), its label taking the accent as it passes, and its settings rise into place as the last group's fade, up from below for a group further down the list and down from above for one higher up.
- Tablet mode, phase 3: on a screen wider than a phone, Now Playing lays itself out for the larger cover. On a wide window the cover sits on one side, as large as the height allows, with the song, its controls and the actions centred on the other; on a tall one it stays one column, kept to a phone's width and centred. Near square, whichever gives the larger cover wins, and it only switches once the other is clearly larger, so resizing a window doesn't flicker between them. Phones are unchanged.
- In two columns, Lyrics, Up next, Song info and Auto DJ open over the controls' side, beside the cover rather than over it.
- Beside the nav rail, the mini player now sits under the rail at the foot of the screen rather than centred along it: as narrow as the rail and standing on end like it, with the cover, the progress line under it and play/pause. The rail stays centred in the room above the mini player's place whether or not there's a song, so it doesn't move as music starts or stops. Now Playing still grows out of it, and folds back into it. The pages no longer keep room at their foot for the mini player.
- The mini player under the rail is taller (176dp), with its cover set further down from the round top so its corners keep clear of the curve, and its progress is now a ring round play/pause, filling clockwise from the top as the song plays, in place of the line under the cover.
- Grids have up to 6 columns on a tablet or an open foldable (still 4 on a phone), and each kind of screen keeps its own column count: until changed there, 4 on a foldable, 6 on a tablet held wide and 4 held tall, and a phone's as before. The list or grid choice is still one for all.
- With a menu open beside the rail, the dim now reaches evenly to the foot of the screen (a lighter band showed where the page fades out at the bottom).
- Folding or unfolding (or turning a tablet), the app shows on the other screen straight away in its new layout, with no fade or settling. The nav bar or rail is simply there (it no longer slides in), and the covers already on screen stay in while they load again at their new size, where the placeholder showed for a moment. Everything carries across as it was: the tab, the page open and how far it's scrolled (a grid keeps the same albums at the top, in however many columns), Now Playing and any panel open over it, and in Settings the group picked in two columns, which the phone's one column opens at, and scrolling there picks it back.
- New setting, Appearance → Keep nav rail still (on by default, wider screens only): on, the rail stays in one place as music starts and stops; off, it moves up to stay centred above the mini player while something plays, as before. Dimmed on a phone, with a hint that it's only for wider screens.
- Tablet mode, phase 4: on a tablet or an open foldable (700dp and wider), Home, Library and Search open pages in a second pane: with nothing open, the tab's own page has the whole width; opening an album, artist, playlist, genre or "see all" eases that page to the side as the opened one slides in beside it, and closing the last eases it back to the whole width. Picking another item replaces what's open rather than stacking on it, links inside it (an album's artist) still open on top, and back steps back through them to the hint. It's the same back stack as the one-pane layout, so turning or folding keeps what's open, and the side page keeps its place. Grids at the side use fewer columns where covers would come out narrower than 130dp.
- Covers are decoded at a few fixed sizes (each about a quarter larger than the last) rather than exactly the size they're laid out at, so a grid easing between widths reuses what's in memory instead of flashing placeholders as it goes.
- Labels in the nav bar and the Library's tab bar no longer get cut off: the selected tab grows as wide as the longest label needs (taking the room from the tabs showing just their icons, which keep enough for them), where its fixed share left a long label, or any label on a narrow bar such as the Library beside an opened page, fading out part way.
- Two columns only in landscape: the list with what's opened beside it, and two-column Settings, now appear only when a tablet or an open foldable is held the wide way round. Held tall, pages are one column beside the rail, as on a smaller tablet. Turning the screen keeps what's open either way.
- Tablet mode, phase 6 (keyboard and mouse, as on a tablet's keyboard cover or DeX): Space plays or pauses, Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right go to the previous and next song, and Esc goes back. Space in Search's field still types a space and presses a focused button, and Ctrl+arrows there still move by word. A mouse over a button without a ripple (the player's controls, the nav bar's, rail's and tab bars' tabs) lights it faintly; the right button on a song row, an album or artist card or a settings row does what a long press does; and Shift with the wheel scrolls Home's rows sideways.
- Samsung DeX: the app now knows when it's in DeX, from its window being on a screen without touch (One UI 8 no longer has the desktop mode field Samsung once documented; older One UI's still counts). There, the mini player under the rail sits 24dp higher, clear of the taskbar and the window's edge, with the rail still centred above it; the two panes split the width evenly rather than a narrow list beside a very wide page; and Now Playing always shows its actions as a row of buttons for the mouse, not the capsule stack (Stack Now Playing buttons is dimmed there, with a hint saying why).
- Now Playing in two columns (landscape on a tablet, an open foldable or DeX): the song and its controls and the actions below them now have even room above, between and below, where the controls sat centred and the actions were pushed to the foot with a wide gap between.
- Text is larger on a tablet (15%) and in DeX (20%), where it read small from further away; on top of the phone's own font size setting. Phones and foldables (open or shut) are unchanged.
- Now Playing in two columns: the right side now runs the cover's height, the song at the cover's top and the actions at its foot, with the seek bar and controls between (and as tall as its content needs where the cover is shorter, as on a foldable), where it was all bunched in the top half with the actions alone below.
- Now Playing in two columns: the song, the seek bar, the transport and the actions are spread evenly down the cover's height. On a tablet held wide and in DeX, that side is also 20% larger, its text, buttons and spacing alike (the collapse button stays as it is).
- Now Playing's format and output lines no longer cut their text off where it's larger (DeX, a tablet, a large font size on the phone): their rows and tiles are sized with the text rather than fixed, so "FLAC" and the lines beside it show whole.
- Settings' pop-up menus (Theme, Language, Glass and the rest) are now a little narrower than the row they open from and centred on it, on every screen: inside the group's card in two-column Settings, where they reached across and past the page, and in line with the cards on a phone. Add to playlist, opened from a song or an album, keeps its width across the page.
- The nav rail widens to fit its longest label where text is larger (DeX, a large font size), where "Settings" was cut to "Set…"; the pages and the mini player under it move over to match.
- In DeX the nav rail stands taller, each tab 92dp rather than 68dp, as a window there has height to spare.
- In DeX the nav rail's icons are larger too, 32dp rather than 24dp, to suit its taller tabs.
- With a Settings menu open beside the rail, a darker band no longer shows along the foot of the screen: the page's dim now stops exactly where the chrome's own dim takes over, where the two overlapped since the mini player moved under the rail.
- Play buttons (where play, shuffle and queue go as a page scrolls) now defaults to Scroll with the page, rather than Pin at the top. A choice already made in Settings is kept.
- Frosted glass with something moving behind it (Now Playing's scrolling title and seek bar under Lyrics, Up next, Song info or Auto DJ) no longer shimmers. Haze is updated from 1.7.3 to 2.0.1, which takes care of redrawing the blur whenever what's behind it changes. Haze 1.7.3 built a new blur layer every time it drew. The upgrade also needs Kotlin 2.4.20 (from 2.2.10) and the Compose BOM 2026.09.00 (Compose UI 1.10.4 to 1.12.1); blurring is now in the separate `haze-blur` library. The glass is meant to look the same: as translucent, as blurred and with the same grain. The panel cards and Settings menus blur at full size once open and still, and from a copy half the size while they grow or shrink (a third before: Haze 2 goes no lower than half).
- Library with a page open beside it (two panes, on a tablet or an open foldable held wide) keeps its own list or grid choice and column count, apart from when it has the whole screen. Before, the two shared one setting, so it could not be tuned for the side pane. This covers the Library's own tabs (Artists, Albums, Playlists); an artist's or genre's albums, which open beside it, keep the full-screen setting. The side pane also stopped taking in changes while a page was open beside it (it counted as hidden), so it kept showing the full-screen count; it now stays live while on screen.
- In two columns, a menu open over one pane (the Library's view options) no longer leaves a dark bar along the foot of the other pane: the chrome's dim now sits only under the pane the menu dims, and the rail.
- The Library view options slider in two columns follows the finger on its own and changes the column count only as it snaps to another step, so it can no longer stick.
- Dragging a page past its top or bottom now moves the whole list, and the Play buttons row with it, instead of the system stretch (which only the list drew, so the row stayed behind). Pulling is held back to half the finger, and it springs back on release.
- With the Play buttons pinned at the top, their capsule now stands right beside the back button (level with it, to its right) instead of under it.
- The queue and where you were in it are remembered after the app is closed or the phone restarts. Opening the app shows the same queue, song and position, paused; and pressing play on the media controls (Samsung's panel, the lock screen or a headset button) picks up from there. The place is saved every few seconds while playing, and on pause, skip, seek, shuffle and repeat changes.
